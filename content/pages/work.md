Summary: Engineering leadership and hands-on engineering at Norvia Labs, Moveworks, LinkedIn, Postman and Booking.com: platforms, data, developer tools and AI agents.

Selected work from 18-odd years of hands-on engineering and engineering leadership, across architecture, technical decision-making, and delivery. Most of it is platforms: the things other teams build on.

<section class="work-card" id="norvia-labs">
  <h2><a href="https://www.norvialabs.com" rel="noopener noreferrer">Norvia Labs</a></h2>
  <p class="work-role">Founder · 2025 – present</p>
  <p class="work-outcome">Building open-source infrastructure for putting AI agents to work inside limits people set. The first product is <a href="https://github.com/NorviaLabs/forge" rel="noopener noreferrer">Forge</a>, a terminal AI coding environment in Rust with sandboxed tool execution, approvals, durable sessions and isolated Git worktrees. I built it on my own: 1,500+ commits and 21 releases so far.</p>
</section>

<section class="work-card" id="moveworks">
  <h2><a href="https://www.moveworks.com" rel="noopener noreferrer">Moveworks</a></h2>
  <p class="work-role">Engineering Manager · Analytics and real-time integrations · 2024 – 2025</p>
  <p class="work-outcome">Led two teams in Bangalore, 17 engineers in all. One built the analytics behind Moveworks’ agentic AI assistant, the Data API, and Surya, which we rebuilt from a backend service into a full-stack platform. The other built the real-time system that crawls customer data sources and keeps every record in sync, which meant incremental fetch, tenant isolation and a webhook system of our own.</p>
</section>

<section class="work-card" id="linkedin">
  <h2><a href="https://www.linkedin.com" rel="noopener noreferrer">LinkedIn</a></h2>
  <p class="work-role">Engineering Manager · Enterprise data platform and integrations · 2022 – 2023</p>
  <p class="work-outcome">Led two teams of 14 engineers that moved 1,500 sales datasets and 25+ go-to-market teams onto one data platform. P95 data freshness went from 72 hours to 6, and data errors from over 10% to 0.5%.</p>
</section>

<section class="work-card" id="postman">
  <h2><a href="https://www.postman.com" rel="noopener noreferrer">Postman</a></h2>
  <p class="work-role">Engineering Manager · Runtime · 2020 – 2022</p>
  <p class="work-outcome">Led the Runtime team, the engine that sends every request and runs every script, as it grew from 5 to 11 engineers. We took Postman to the browser (13M monthly active users in its first six months) and beyond HTTP, with WebSocket, Socket.IO and gRPC support.</p>
</section>

<section class="work-card" id="booking">

</section>
