Summary: Engineer, engineering leader, reader of too many books at once, based in Bangalore.

I'm Mohit Ranka. I work as an engineer and an engineering leader — fifteen-odd years across Booking.com, Postman, LinkedIn, and Moveworks — and I've spent most of my adult life in Bangalore.

The job is one part of it. Most of my attention goes to trying to understand how things work: philosophy and religion, psychology, systems thinking, strategy, economics, politics, and technology. I don't have a unifying theory. I have a reading list, which is the next best thing.

I build software for the usual reasons: curiosity, the quiet satisfaction of making something, occasionally because I need the thing to exist, and mostly to learn. [Forge](/projects/) is the current example.

## Outside work {#interests}

Chess, music, sitcoms, movies, books, and sports, in an order that changes by the week.

I like learning new skills, usually by giving myself a short, specific goal and seeing how far I get before the next one turns up.

The books I keep coming back to are in the [Library](/library/). The longer answer to who I am is probably in the [writing](/blog/).
