# Surface brief: One person, multiple worlds

Scope: every page of the Pelican site. Each URL renders a complete Just Read page (Mode: Read). Three optional worlds re-present the same pages (Mode: Experience), opened from the homepage panorama or the "Visit a world" control and remembered per visitor.
Audience/job: readers of the essays, and people getting to know Mohit. Not a portfolio, résumé or funnel (PRODUCT.md).
User requests, verbatim: "Rebuild mohitranka.com — One Person, Multiple Worlds" brief, including "Do not deploy or publish without explicit approval." Answers: Projects "Forge only, for now"; Music "Original, composed in code (Recommended)"; About "Draft from the brief; I'll review (Recommended)"; Cadence "Approve designs, then build all (Recommended)"; sketches "Approve all, build it".
Content commitments: the same six sections in every world.
- About: My Apartment / front door / Traveler's Home.
- Writing: The Daily Commit / writing desk / Library of Stories.
- Work: Business District / computer / The Citadel.
- Projects: The Garage / workbench / Craftsman's Forge.
- Library: Bookstore / bookshelf / Ancient Archives.
- Contact: Post Office / telephone / Messenger's Outpost.

Jokes live in the environments. Copy states only facts from the site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One person, three genuinely different worlds, one set of pages. Content first: a world is a way of browsing, never a gate. Refuses both the plain résumé site and the 3D splash that hides the reading.
OWN-WORLD:
- Just Read: paper #fbf8f2, ink #1d1c1a, accent #b54a17, with Source Serif 4 for reading and Inter for UI.
- Side Quest City: a dusk-purple and sodium-orange low-poly Bangalore in Three.js, with a Big Shoulders HUD in yellow #ffe066.
- Apartment 404: a mustard, plum and rose SVG sitcom set, lettered in Righteous, with a remote control.
- The Unfinished Atlas: a parchment SVG map in iron-gall ink #3b2a18 with a red road #a83a2a, lettered in IM Fell English and SC.
STORY: A visitor lands on any URL as Just Read. From the homepage panorama or "Visit a world" they pick a world. That world opens at the place for the current page, with the page in its reader. Moving between places keeps URLs canonical. "Just Read" returns to the plain page at the same position. Returning visitors open in their last world.
FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Home (Just Read): name, a one-line introduction, then the three-world panorama.
- City: the full city, a location readout, quick travel and a minimap.
- Apartment: the whole set, a title card and the remote.
- Atlas: the map, cartouche, legend and zoom controls.
- All worlds: HUD pills top-right for world, music, theme and Just Read.
FORM: The user pinned the three worlds in the brief. Sketches 01–05 in .impeccable/mocks/worlds were approved ("Approve all, build it").
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished. This build ends with the finish review, DESIGN.md, the validation report and the list of incomplete requirements. Nothing is pushed or deployed without explicit approval.
