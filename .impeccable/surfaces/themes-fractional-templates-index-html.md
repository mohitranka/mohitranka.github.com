# Surface brief: The Unfinished Atlas, with Traveler's Home on it

Scope: every page of the Pelican site. Each URL renders a complete Just Read page (Mode: Read). One optional world, The Unfinished Atlas, re-presents the same pages (Mode: Experience). The map is the home screen and holds the outward sections. Traveler's Home is a cottage on the map that holds the personal ones. Visitors open it from the homepage invitation or the header's "Open the map", and the choice is remembered.
Audience/job: readers of the essays, and people getting to know Mohit. Not a portfolio, résumé or funnel (PRODUCT.md).
User requests, verbatim (latest first):
- "Its an odd way to merge map and apartment. Typically apartments exist inside the map. Also map is medivial and apartment is modern". Answer: "All fantasy (Recommended)".
- "For music, 1. Genrate 7 or 8 more songs. 2. Make them available from the "world" itself ( Radio or medival music instrument from the apartment, we need to figure out what needs to be done for the map.) 3. By default keep the music on."
- Earlier: dropping the city, rotating the music, and the original multi-world brief.

Content commitments: each section lives in one place.
- Map: Writing (Library of Stories), Work (The Citadel), Projects (Craftsman's Forge).
- Cottage: About (front door), Library (shelves), Contact (messenger's letterbox).
- Shortcuts in the cottage lead out to the map places: the writing desk, the letter from the Citadel, the workbench.
- The Minstrel's Camp on the map and the lute in the cottage open the songbook.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One person's world drawn as an old map, with his home on it. Content first: the map is a way of browsing, never a gate.
OWN-WORLD:
- Just Read: paper #fbf8f2, ink #1d1c1a, accent #b54a17, with Source Serif 4 and Inter.
- The world: parchment #efe2c4, iron-gall ink #3b2a18, road red #a83a2a, lettered in IM Fell English and SC.
- The cottage: lime plaster, oak timber, field stone and hearth light, in the same lettering.
STORY: A visitor lands on any URL as Just Read. If they unroll the map, they arrive on the map, or in the cottage when the page is About, Library or Contact. Traveler's Home zooms into the cottage; "Back to the map" or Escape returns. "Just Read" returns to the plain page.
MUSIC: eleven original tracks rotate (210 seconds each, 4-second crossfade), on by default in the world. Sound starts at the first click or tap. "Off" is remembered, and Just Read is silent. The songbook picks songs.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished. Nothing is pushed without explicit approval.
