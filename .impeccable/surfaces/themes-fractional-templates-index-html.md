# Surface brief: whole site (home, writing, essays, work, library, contact)

Scope: the entire Pelican theme `themes/fractional`. Mode: Read (essays are the product); the homepage is a profile in the same world.
Audience/job: readers of the essays; people checking who Mohit is. No pitch, no job-seeking (see PRODUCT.md).
Reference bar: maggieappleton.com for completeness of content types and an identity sentence; not its look.
Content commitments: identity sentence "Mohit builds software, leads engineering teams, reads widely, and writes it all down here." Library from Goodreads: 7 favourites first, then everything read grouped by year, no ratings. Homepage previews latest essays, work, and favourite books. Margin notes state only facts from the site, never invented opinions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A writer's notebook kept in public, where the opening sentence is the navigation: each verb (builds, leads, reads, writes) links to its section. Refuses the bare name-tagline-post-list default.
OWN-WORLD: A plain paper sheet (#fbfaf6; night #15171c) laid on a dot-grid desk (#efede6 / #0d0f13) that shows at the sheet's edges; blue-black ink #1c1d22; fountain-pen blue #2348a8 (night #93adff) for every link, underline and margin note. Literata for headings and reading, Bricolage Grotesque for small labels and controls, Kalam only for short handwritten margin notes and year markers. Real book covers are the only imagery.
STORY: A visitor reads one sentence and knows who Mohit is and where everything lives; they open an essay, a project or a book and keep reading, the way you'd leaf through someone's notebook.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim header on the sheet (name left; Writing, Work, Library, Contact, theme toggle right). The identity sentence in Literata at display size (about 56px desktop) across two-thirds of the sheet, verbs underlined in pen blue; a handwritten margin note at the right edge with the first entry's year and title; a quiet line with the 15+ years and Forge beneath; the Writing section starting above the fold.
FORM: Writer's Notebook, the user's pick from the safer-register lineup on the decision page (answer file .impeccable/questions/80ece173.answer.json, optionId "notebook"), confirmed in the terminal as "Notebook + Maggie content" after the build first missed it.
